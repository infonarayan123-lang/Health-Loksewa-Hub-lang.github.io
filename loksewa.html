Nepal's public service exams are run by the Public Service Commission (Lok Sewa Aayog) at the federal level and by a Provincial Public Service Commission in each of the seven provinces. These links open the official websites.

Questions

Loksewa questions, answered

Where can I find the official syllabus?
Open the website of the commission that runs your exam and look for the syllabus section (पाठ्यक्रम). Use the official syllabus, not a copy from somewhere else, because it can change.
Are federal and provincial Loksewa exams the same?
No. The federal Public Service Commission and each Provincial Public Service Commission run their own exams. Syllabus, calendar and vacancies can differ, so check the commission for your post.
How do I know when the next exam is?
Check the annual exam calendar and the notices on the official website. Dates can change, so look again close to the exam.
Does Health Loksewa replace the official notice?
No. We are an independent study app. The official notice and syllabus on the commission's website always come first.
