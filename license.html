Questions

Licence exam questions, answered

What is a licence exam?
A licence exam is held by a professional council. Passing it lets you register with the council and practise your profession.
Is a licence exam the same as a Loksewa exam?
No. Loksewa exams are for government service jobs. Licence exams are for registering to practise your profession. Many health workers need both.
Where do I find my council's syllabus and exam dates?
On the official website of your council. Look for the syllabus, the annual exam calendar and the notices, and check them again close to the exam.
