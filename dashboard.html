Use it on your iPhone or laptop

  1. iPhone (Safari): open the app, tap the Share button, then tap Add to Home Screen.
  2. Laptop (Chrome or Edge): open the app, then press Ctrl + D to bookmark it.
  3. Android: the app opens in the browser too. A Play Store app is coming soon.

To sign out, tap your name in the top bar.
