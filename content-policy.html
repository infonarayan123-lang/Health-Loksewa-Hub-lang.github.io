How we prepare and check our content

Who makes our questions and notes, how answers are checked, and what to do if you find a mistake.

Last updated: 27 October 2026

Who prepares it

The Health Loksewa Hub team prepares the questions and notes. Our main sources are standard textbooks.

How answers are checked

Before students see a question, its answer is checked against a textbook by the person who prepared it. We do not claim that every answer is perfect. If you find a mistake, please tell us.

Past questions and model questions

Real past exam questions are kept in their own separate sets (Past Questions). The questions in the practice sections are model questions prepared by us.

About AI

Our questions, answers and notes are not written by AI. The app has an AI helper chat for students. Its answers can be wrong, so always check important facts in your textbook or the official source. If an AI answer looks wrong or unsuitable, tap “Report this answer” in the app.

If you find a mistake

In the app, tap the report button on a question. On this website, use “Found a mistake? Tell us” on the Question of the day page. We check every report. Please allow about 24 hours. If the question is wrong, we correct it.

The report button does not send us your email address. If you would like an answer from us, email us the question and your email address.

Official information

For dates, rules, vacancies and syllabus, always check the official notice of the Public Service Commission or the relevant body. Our pages are for study only and are not medical advice.
