Questions

Frequently asked questions

Is Health Loksewa free?
You can start for free. A Premium plan unlocks extra material and higher limits. Plans are shown inside the app.
Which exams can I prepare for?
Public Health Officer (PHO), Health Assistant, Staff Nurse, AHW, ANM, BPH License and HA License. Some exams have more questions than others, and new material is added regularly.
Is this an official Public Service Commission app?
No. Health Loksewa is an independent study app and is not affiliated with the Public Service Commission (Lok Sewa Aayog). Always check the official notices for exam rules and syllabus.
Does it work without internet?
The app opens without internet and your saved items stay on your phone, but loading new questions, the AI tools and live features need an internet connection.
How is my information used?
Please read our Privacy Policy for exactly what we collect and why. You can also delete your account at any time from the delete-account page.
Can I rely on the app alone to pass?
Practice helps a lot, but no app can guarantee a result. Use Health Loksewa together with the official syllabus and your own study plan.
