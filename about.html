About Health Loksewa

An independent study app for health students preparing for Nepal's Loksewa and licence exams.

What Health Loksewa is

Health Loksewa is a study app for health students in Nepal who are preparing for Loksewa and licence exams. It brings MCQ practice, timed mock tests, past questions, notes and study tools into one place, and it is free to start.

How we work

  • We are an independent app. We are not the Public Service Commission and we are not a council.
  • We write our own explanations and summaries, and we link to the official source for notices, syllabus and rules.
  • We cannot guarantee any exam result. We aim to help you practise well and learn from your mistakes.
  • Study material is for exam preparation and is not medical advice.
  • The practice questions are for study. They are not copies of any official exam paper.
  • We never sell your personal data.

Your privacy

We explain what we collect and why in our Privacy Policy, and you can delete your account at any time on the delete-account page. Our Terms of Use and Community Rules are public too.
