Is Health Loksewa free?
You can start for free. A Premium plan unlocks extra material and higher limits. Plans are shown inside the app.
Which exams can I prepare for?
Public Health Officer (PHO), Health Assistant, Staff Nurse, AHW, ANM, BPH License and HA License. Some exams have more questions than others, and new material is added regularly. Each exam has its own page: choose one in the Exams section of the home page.
How do I open the app and put it on my phone?
Tap Open the app. To keep it on your phone like an app: on Android (Chrome) open the app link, tap the three dots menu and choose “Add to Home screen”. On iPhone (Safari) open the app link, tap the Share button and choose “Add to Home Screen”. The steps are also in the Get the app section of the home page. No store is needed.
How do I sign in?
Open the app and follow the sign-in screen. You can sign in with your email and password, or with Google. Signing in keeps your progress with your own account.
Is this an official Public Service Commission app?
No. Health Loksewa is an independent study app and is not affiliated with the Public Service Commission (Lok Sewa Aayog). Always check the official notices for exam rules and syllabus.
Where do I find the official syllabus and notices?
On the official websites. Our Loksewa Aayog page lists the federal Public Service Commission and the seven provincial commissions. Our Licence page lists the Nepal Health Professional Council and the other health councils. Please always check dates and rules there.
Can I rely on the app alone to pass?
Practice helps a lot, but no app can guarantee a result. Use Health Loksewa together with the official syllabus and your own study plan.
How do I report a mistake in a question?
In the app, tap Report a mistake under a question after you answer it, or on a card in the test review. On this website use “Found a mistake in this question? Tell us” under the Question of the day, or write to us on the contact page. We cannot promise that every question is perfect, so please also check important facts in your textbooks and in official documents.
How does the question of the day work?
A new question appears every day (Kathmandu time) on the home page and the question page. Tap an answer to see the right one with a short explanation. You can share the question as text or as a picture. The answer is only in the Answer picture, which appears after you have answered.
Can the AI Study Help make mistakes?
Yes. AI can be wrong. Use it to understand ideas, then check important facts in your textbooks or in official documents. In the app you can report an AI answer that looks wrong with “Report this AI answer”.
Does it work without internet?
The app opens without internet and your saved items stay on your phone, but loading new questions, the AI tools and live features need an internet connection.
How is my information used, and can I delete my account?
Please read our Privacy Policy for exactly what we collect and why. You can also delete your account at any time from the delete-account page.
How can I contact you?
Use the contact page: your email app opens with your message ready to send. You can also write to healthloksewa026@gmail.com.
