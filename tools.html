Calculators

BMI calculator

Body mass index from your weight and height, for adults.

Due date calculator

Estimated date of delivery from the first day of the last menstrual period.

Sample size calculator

How many people to study, for a survey that estimates a proportion (the usual formula, with the finite population correction).

Risk and odds (2 by 2 table)

Relative risk, odds ratio, risk difference and number needed to treat from a 2 by 2 table.

Body surface area (BSA)

Body surface area from weight and height, with the Mosteller formula.

IV drip rate (for learning)

Drops per minute from the volume, the time and the drop factor of the set. For study and exam practice only.

IV drip rate is only for learning and exams. It must never be used to treat a patient. Always follow the doctor's order, the hospital protocol and the infusion set instructions, and ask a senior nurse. Sample size, risk and odds use the same formulas as the Health Loksewa app, with the standard simplifications; real research needs a statistician's advice.

These tools give general estimates for learning and are not medical advice. BMI is for adults and does not apply to children or pregnancy. Please talk to a health worker about your own health. More calculators (statistics, epidemiology, salary and more) are inside the app.
